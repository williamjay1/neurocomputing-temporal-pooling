1. Endpoint average MSE equals mean error squared plus signed-detail error squared. Innovation risk eta is the sum of the two coordinate innovation variances, not half that sum or twice it. With SD .1 per coordinate, eta=.02.
2. R_U=(alpha-1)^2 W+beta^2 sigma_A^2+eta follows from M=U+(alpha-1)D+epsilon_C and E[A|U]=0. R_C, R_abs and R_raw are consistent with their available information. The sign symmetry argument applies to independent symmetric A; it is not an optimizer assertion.
3. Positive Gaussian conditional variance gives W>0. Cov(E[D|U],U)=V and total variance give W<V. The separate affine-estimation upper bound W<=V sigma_C^2/(sigma_C^2+V) is valid. Applying the increasing function sqrt(W)/(sqrt(W)+sqrt(V)) gives the stated alpha_star bound. Unique crossing is restricted to [0,1]; the other quadratic root is negative.
4. The finite-moment extension requires independent C and sign-symmetric A, finite second moments, V>0 and W>0. The innovations retain their stated independent zero-mean condition. C need not have zero mean. Independent nonzero variances alone do not give W>0 for arbitrary discrete distributions. This is a standard projection extension and not a new Blackwell result.
5. The truncated-normal conditional variance uses lambda=phi(z)/Phi(z) and the correct bracket 1-z lambda-lambda^2. At the executed 192-node grids, the most negative z values for sigma_A=.25/1/2 were -6.49323/-18.93061/-23.94513. The minimum variance brackets were .02088194/.00274476/.00172609; none was negative or required a clamp. Log-CDF stabilizes the inverse Mills ratio; it does not by itself certify variance subtraction in every arbitrarily extreme tail. The paper should report the actual grid checks and quadrature agreement, without a universal numerical-stability claim.
6. Direct versus signed-Haar input is an invertible change of coordinates, and W_H=W_direct Q^T gives equal initial functions for the actual orthogonal Q. Adam is not rotationally invariant. Neither equal parameter counts nor initial compensation makes noninvertible adapters have equal function classes.
